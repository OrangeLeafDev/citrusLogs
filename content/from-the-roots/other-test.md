# Game Description (GameJolt)

Multiple stories about different experiences in a world where anything can come to life. This is...

![From The Roots](png/logo2.png)

A story-driven turn-based RPG all about a world where anything (literally) can become alive and emerge sentient just like any other person. From something as dead as a rock, to things filled with life like fauna and flora. Robots May/May Not Be Included*

----------

![# Features](png/heading1.png)

-   Story-driven plotline with 6 main acts and 4 3 spinoff acts! Play as multiple characters! The perspective you'll play depends on which act you are in.
-   Citrus.
-   A new yet familiar take on turn-based combat! Unique character-based special abilities! Take advantage of your environment to gain an advantage against your opponent. Otherwise, they'll be able to do the same to you!
-   Turnip...?
-   Your choices do indeed matter! Your actions determine which of the multiple endings in the main story you'll get, and your actions throughout the entire game all come together in the final act.
-   Cactus!
-   A meta-narrative on the process of creation, and how ideas come together into something tangible and beautiful.
-   A game purely driven by passion! (and maybe something cool to share :])
